// MORE ABOUT CALLR

Before
the demo.

Can we start with recorded-call analysis?

Yes. The analytics module accepts recordings of inbound and outbound calls. CallR transcribes the conversation and reviews it against the selected criteria.

Who sets the review standards?

Your head of sales. We first translate your company’s methodology into criteria. Your sales leader can then update the agents’ review rules independently.

What happens after a conversation?

CallR summarizes the conversation, captures commitments, creates follow-up tasks and returns a call review based on your checklist.

How can we see the product?

Contact us on Telegram or by phone. We’ll demonstrate call handling, task creation and call-quality reviews using a scenario relevant to your business.
